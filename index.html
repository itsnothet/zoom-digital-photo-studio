About the studio

Steady, professional photography, every time

Zoom Digital Photo Studio works with individuals, families, couples and businesses across Ahmedabad — from a same-day passport photo to a full wedding weekend. Every booking gets the same attention: careful lighting, steady composition, and a final set of images the client is genuinely happy to keep.

The studio handles documentation photography, portraits, event coverage and product photography for catalogues and websites, backed by in-house printing, framing and album design — so a shoot doesn't end at the camera.

  • In-house printing, framing and album design
